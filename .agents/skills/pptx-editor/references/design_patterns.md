# Presentation Design Patterns & Best Practices

## 1. Information Density & Typography
- **Slide Titles**: 20-28 pt bold, high-contrast headline.
- **Section Kickers**: 10-12 pt uppercase tracking, accent color (e.g., `#574BFF`).
- **Body & Bullets**: 12-16 pt, line spacing 1.15-1.25x.
- **Table Data**: 9-11 pt, tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) for currency and metrics.

## 2. Color Palette Harmony
- **Background**: Dark Mode (`#0B0F17` / `#150F29`) or Light Mode (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation**: `#211C36` or `#1E293B` for card containers.
- **Primary Accent**: `#574BFF` (Indigo) for primary focus.
- **Secondary Accent**: `#F7BC69` (Amber) for warnings, `#10B981` (Emerald) for active status.

## 3. Image & Diagram Framing
- Maintain exact aspect ratios to avoid stretching or squash.
- Provide uniform margins (minimum 0.5 inches from slide edges).
- Align screenshots with adjacent text columns and data tables.
